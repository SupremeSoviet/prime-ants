# Slice 1: wild Lasius niger colonies

## Usable result and release status

**The slice implements one wild species from natural founding through physical food collection and worker production.** Queens excavate a small two-high stair nest, carry soil to a mound, seal it and fund the first clutch from a finite reserve. Real eggs, larvae and cocoons produce pale young workers; workers mature, open the entrance, forage, deliver visible cargo, nurse the colony, share sweetness and perform bounded expansion. Adults need food; workers age, killed ants stay lost and queen death ends new laying. The current cap is **30 adults per colony, including the queen**. This is an early colony simulation, with conservative site checks and physical work rather than direct player job control.

**The candidate is usable for qualified evaluation; final release verification remains pending T30.** The latest completed unfiltered acceptance is **T28: 15 unit / 201 server / 26 model**, with all prior case names retained and production helpers excluded. T28 fixed nursery creation overwriting an occupied torch cell; genuine tick-driven before/after tests prove preservation and paid resumption after explicit removal. T29 changes documentation and the external closure helper only. Its added helper changes the acceptance-input inventory, so T28 is the latest completed build, not a newly verified T29 build. T30 must run a fresh complete build and test its exact resulting JAR in a clean ordinary client.

Natural placement/founding and protein-funded growth were observed in earlier versions, often accelerated. One bounded historical source run kept healthy colonies and reached 30 adults across two colonies. **Continuing sustainability on the final candidate is not established**: recent income could fall below commitments, crowding could block emergence, and apparently suitable terrain could still stall a colony. At defaults, each brood stage takes 12,000 loaded ticks (ten minutes at 20 TPS); the first workers require roughly thirty minutes after laying plus excavation/travel/care. Unloaded chunks, singleplayer pause and a closed world do not advance the colony; offline catch-up is absent. Daylight commands change lighting, not biological age.

Players can watch, use the worker-opened stairs, drop sweetness/protein and add supported lighting without occupying work/component cells. Actual harm provokes local worker defense. Historical T23 demonstrates entry, food delivery with immediate retreat and a bite. T28 demonstrates collection/nursing and a living egg, but **its immediate ordinary retreat failed** and **20,075/20,000 ticks exceeded the bound by 75**. The gallery has three accepted current views and a fourth historical queen image prominently labelled **T25 — previous model revision**. Current queen visual acceptance, ordinary gloss/terraces and rendered stop/restart quality remain incomplete.

Historical T27 dedicated-server measurement of two colonies/30 adults gives mean/median/p95/max **11.027/10.670/14.315/21.832 ms**, zero of 2,400 samples above 50 ms, approximately 20 TPS. It used a diagnostic dimension, loading tickets and persisted work20/brood100; it is not a fresh-world client benchmark. Whole-world reloads passed for observed state, including identity, food and brood progress. Nonempty cargo/custody, terminal-ledger restoration and abrupt-crash durability remain unobserved. A previous Java/C2 crash and dev-only JIT recovery remain historical reliability qualifications; one clean predecessor startup is not a universal fix.

Candidate: [build/libs/prime_ants-0.1.0.jar](../build/libs/prime_ants-0.1.0.jar), SHA-256 `6cab3264ecf1e3c34ce7a3edcfc5ef2937eba5b845e8bce890f91fd68692f367`. Install with **Minecraft 26.3, Java 25.0.3+9, Loader 0.19.5 and API 0.161.0+26.3**; build tool Loom remains 1.18.2. [EN/RU installation and play](../README.md) · [Concise player guide](player-guide.md) · [Qualified four-image gallery](screenshots/t28-release.md) · [Exact T30 handoff](t30-release-handoff.md).

**Recommended next direction after delivery:** unattended colony reliability under ordinary player loading and default timing, concentrating on nutrition, routing/crowding and unload/reload boundaries. Established-colony generation, nuptial flights, active fruit harvesting, further species and offline catch-up remain deferred; they are not promises for this release.

## Default timing and accelerated saves

These values come from [SimulationTimeScale](../src/main/java/dev/primeants/time/SimulationTimeScale.java), [QueenFounding](../src/main/java/dev/primeants/founding/QueenFounding.java), [BroodPile](../src/main/java/dev/primeants/brood/BroodPile.java), [BroodRecord](../src/main/java/dev/primeants/brood/BroodRecord.java) and [AdultLife](../src/main/java/dev/primeants/entity/AdultLife.java), read in T29. They supersede approximate design proposals in [00-decisions](00-decisions.md) when describing what is shipped.

| Event/policy | Fresh default, loaded ticks | At 20 TPS; qualifications |
|---|---:|---|
| Founding work cadence | 200 | 10 s per eligible action; movement/site preparation adds time, not a ten-second whole nest. Work multiplier defaults to 1. |
| Egg, larva, cocoon stage | 12,000 each | 10 min each; about 30 min from laying to emergence if care, food, habitat and space permit. Brood multiplier defaults to 1. |
| Callow maturation | 4,800 | 4 min after emergence. |
| Eligible later laying cadence | 1,200 | 1 min; capacity, recent real intake, stores and commitments still gate laying. Nursery has three slots. |
| Worker age threshold | 144,000 | 2 h before age damage begins, not a guaranteed instant death or queen age limit. |
| Unsupported adult fasting grace | 24,000 | 20 min before starvation damage; damage accrues one health point per 20 eligible ticks. |
| Brood neglect / ready-cocoon waiting bounds | 24,000 each | 20 loaded min of the relevant unmet condition; not normal stage duration. |
| Adult maintenance | 1 sugar per 4 covered ticks | Real food accounting; remaining coverage persists. Reserve-only first-clutch founding is separately exempt. |

The queen's unchanged **39,000** reserve funds three eggs and their larval costs (1,000 + 12,000 per slot); it does not regenerate unlimited population. Worker lifespan/fasting and brood expiry policies are selected/persisted separately from acceleration.

Historical work20/brood100 reduces nominal founding cadence to 10 ticks, stages to 120 and callow maturation to 48. T28 itself ran at ordinary 20 TPS with new work1/brood1 selections, but its source nursery still persisted stage120 and prior callow durations. Resetting JVM multipliers does not rewrite saved durations. Daylight/weather changes used for images never fast-forward these loaded biological clocks. T29 ran no client or biological workload.

Actors occupied **prime_ants_test:placement_native**, with two radius4 loading tickets spanning nest boundaries, view/simulation2/2, no players and retained vanilla entities. Native timing includes normal world/colony work and excludes scheduled sleep and the END diagnostic observer; warm-up and closure serialization are outside the measurement. ## Closure, unresolved issues and final verification

The old ordinary launcher falsely required `All dimensions are saved`, absent in valid 26.3 integrated-server closes. T29's [small reusable verifier](../scripts/verify-client-closure.py) requires a completed normal process wait with exit zero, every declared opening, ordered **Stopping server → Saving players → Saving worlds → all three dimension saves**, then client stopping, settled saved files and a CRC-valid archive matching those files. Requested stopping, isolated phrases, nonzero/forced exits, fatal teardown and partial saves/archives fail. Offline replay accepts T27's one and T28's three openings and rejects 27 negative cases. Historical `normal_close=false` records stay unchanged; retrospective settlement compares today's preserved files/archives and does not invent an original pre-archive scan. [Replay](../../turnloop/directions/prime-ants-slice1/turns/T29/closure-replay-results.json), [corrected launch recipe](../../turnloop/directions/prime-ants-slice1/turns/T29/launch-client.py).

Remaining limitations include strict terrain acceptance, route/crowding stalls, finite food and no automatic flood/enclosure repair; no broad modpack/multiplayer, long-client stability or abrupt-crash acceptance. Historical Java/C2 failure recovered under a **dev-only** exact-method JIT exclusion. One clean predecessor startup leaves universal reliability unproven. T26 transition sampler/teardown runtime recovery remains unverified, with zero retained render samples. Its four original lighting records are **block13/sky12 above the pile**, not sky0. Rejected brood/queen images stay rejected.

T29's queen preflight found no already verified live tick observer/early-close path in the ordinary recipe. Building a new watchdog was not a cheap capture; the authorized unavailable branch was used with **0 starts / 0 openings / 0 new PNG / 0 ticks**, no new archive. The original accepted T25 daylight PNG supplies illustration four unchanged. It cannot complete current queen appearance acceptance; T28's clipped original remains preserved. [Decision record](../../turnloop/directions/prime-ants-slice1/turns/T29/queen-result.json).

**T30 is the final verification and delivery turn:** fresh full build and original freshness/helper gates; exact resulting runtime JAR/hash installed with only API into a clean directory; ordinary fresh survival world and natural placement without dev classes/JIT workaround; corrected closure and settled archive; final README/report status, commit and clean tree; deliver the result and close the slice. No appearance, ecology, performance or persistence campaign is reopened. [Concrete handoff](t30-release-handoff.md).
