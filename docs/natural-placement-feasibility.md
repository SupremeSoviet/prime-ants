## Persisted reservation and bounded work

A normally completed Minecraft save future is not evidence of a write. The raw persisted candidate must match generation authority, seed, dimension, chunk, queen UUID, RESERVED status and a fresh attempt UUID before addFreshEntity. Missing/unreadable/mismatched data prevents insertion and logs an explicit stage/detail. A placement-only CREATE_NEW insertion fence is also written/read back before insertion; successful or uncertain insertion keeps it permanently. Only definite non-insertion plus independently verified PENDING permits release. A surviving RESERVED record loads as terminal INDETERMINATE. The fence also fails closed if a completion ledger is truncated or later missing; this last boundary is implementation inspection, not destructive fault-injection evidence. Filesystem readback proves a readable current-process reservation, **not power-loss durability or atomic entity/ledger persistence**.

Every ready evaluation checkpoints and independently verifies a persisted work counter before calling a preliminary plan or creating a temporary queen. Bounds per candidate are **10 evaluations, 40 preliminary plan calls, 10 temporary queen creations, 10 immediate validations, 10 reservation-write/verifications across restoration and 3 actual insertion calls**. Per level tick: two evaluations, at most eight preliminary calls plus two final calls, two temporary queens, two reservation/insertion attempts.
